# VOICE — which register to write in (read before writing any copy)

This skill produces two very different kinds of text. Use the right register for each. Either way, avoid
flowery, polished "AI" prose: no rhetorical parallelism, no three-beat rhythms, no "the very accident of
history that…", and avoid em-dashes (use periods, commas, colons, or parentheses).

## Register B — LEGAL & ADVOCACY TEXT (most of what this skill renders)
For the jurisdiction pages (summaries, each activity's `why`, restrictions, disclaimers) and the advocacy
kits (`problem` / `ask` / `branch_note` / letter templates).
- **Factual, clear, precise, concise, but complete.** Neutral and informational, like a good government
  FAQ or an encyclopedia entry.
- **Not chatty, warm, friendly, or salesy.** People reading legal information want the facts; warmth and
  flourish confuse them and are not appreciated.
- State the fact, cite the source, stop. Say each thing once.
- Advocacy: lay out the facts and pose the cost-benefit question as the jurisdiction's to decide. Never
  preempt the conclusion ("no risk", "you must allow"); there is some risk, and whether it is outweighed is
  theirs to judge.

## Register A — THE SITE OWNER'S OWN VOICE
For blog posts, "about" / mission pages, and image captions: anything written as the owner or their project.
- Use the **owner's own authentic voice, not a generic AI voice.** Ask the owner for samples of their
  writing (a blog post, a forum reply, a caption) and **model the real samples**: their sentence length,
  vocabulary, warmth, humor, hedges, and quirks. Keep a small corpus of their samples and re-read it before
  drafting.
- In general this voice is warm, personal, plain-worded, first person, with short-to-medium sentences,
  parentheticals for asides, and a clear "why". Honest, never salesy.
- Do not layer flowery prose on top of the owner's voice. If you don't have samples yet, ask for some
  before writing in their name.

## How to decide
Ask: is this neutral legal/factual information (Register B), or the owner speaking about their animal and
mission (Register A)? Factual history inside an owner-voice post can use a neutral Register-B tone for the
facts, then return to the owner's voice for the point.
